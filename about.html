About Employee Leave Management System

The Employee Leave Management System is a web-based application designed to manage employee leave requests easily and efficiently.

Employees can apply for leave, view submitted leave requests and manage their leave records through this system.

System Features

  • Employee Leave Application
  • Employee Leave Details
  • Leave Request Management
  • Dashboard for Leave Information
  • Simple and User-Friendly Interface
